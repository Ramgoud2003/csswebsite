Our featured meeting destinations

Copenhagen

Denmark's compact capital is one of the greenest and most sustainable cities in the world. Reputed to be among the world's happiest cities, Copenhagen is also known for its excellent culinary scene and cutting-edge Danish design. Planners will find plenty of meeting venues in Copenhagen to choose from, plus a wealth of activities, all within walking distance of each other.

Oslo

Norway’s capital city is a cutting-edge destination, providing an appealing backdrop for event planners looking for an exciting location. Meeting venues in Oslo range from large conference centers to small creative spaces, and there are plenty of experiences to keep attendees busy outside the conference room.

READ MORE →

Stockholm

Nicknamed the ‘Venice of the north’, Stockholm is built on 14 islands, with sweeping views across the water in every direction. Stockholm is also a great place to host sustainable events, while the endless experiences on offer will keep delegates entertained all year round.

READ MORE →

Frankfurt

Thanks to a central location, warm hospitality and excellent conference hotels and venues, Frankfurt is a world-class destination for meetings and events. Every year, it hosts a wide range of fairs, conferences and business events.
